A) Trigonometric Formulae Summary

Square of Trigonometric Ratios

\[ \begin{aligned} \sin^2 A + \cos^2 A &= 1 \\ 1 + \tan^2 A &= \sec^2 A \\ 1 + \cot^2 A &= \csc^2 A \end{aligned} \]

Compound Angle Formulae

\[ \begin{aligned} \sin(A \pm B) &= \sin A \cos B \pm \cos A \sin B \\ \cos(A \pm B) &= \cos A \cos B \mp \sin A \sin B \\ \tan(A \pm B) &= \frac{\tan A \pm \tan B}{1 \mp \tan A \tan B} \end{aligned} \]

Double Angle Formulae

\[ \begin{aligned} \sin 2A &= 2 \sin A \cos A \\ \cos 2A &= \cos^2 A - \sin^2 A \\ &= 1 - 2\sin^2 A \\ &= 2\cos^2 A - 1 \\ \tan 2A &= \frac{2\tan A}{1 - \tan^2 A} \\ \sin^2 A &= \frac{1 - \cos 2A}{2} \\ \cos^2 A &= \frac{1 + \cos 2A}{2} \end{aligned} \]

Product-to-sum Formulae

\[ \begin{aligned} \sin A \cos B &= \sin\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) + \sin\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \\ \cos A \cos B &= \cos\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) + \cos\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \\ \sin A \sin B &= -\cos\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) + \cos\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \end{aligned} \]

Sum-to-product Formulae

\[ \begin{aligned} \sin A + \sin B &= 2 \sin\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \\ \sin A - \sin B &= 2 \cos\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) \sin\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \\ \cos A + \cos B &= 2 \cos\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \\ \cos A - \cos B &= -2 \sin\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right) \sin\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right) \end{aligned} \]

Type 1: Reciprocals and Squares of Trigonometric Ratios

Example 1

Prove the following identities.

(a) \(\frac{\tan^4 x - 1}{1 - 2\cos^2 x} = \sec^4 x\)

(b) \(\frac{\csc A + \csc B}{\cot A + \cot B} = \frac{\cot A - \cot B}{\csc A - \csc B}\)

(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\tan^4 x - 1}{1 - 2\cos^2 x} &= \frac{(\tan^2 x + 1)(\tan^2 x - 1)}{1 - 2\cos^2 x} \\ &= \frac{\sec^2 x \left( \frac{\sin^2 x}{\cos^2 x} - 1 \right)}{1 - 2\cos^2 x} \\ &= \frac{\frac{1}{\cos^2 x} \left( \frac{\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x}{\cos^2 x} \right)}{\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x - 2\cos^2 x} \\ &= \frac{\sec^4 x (\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x)}{\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x} \\ &= \sec^4 x \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Key: Where there are a lot of squares in the question, you can make use of either the square identities or sum of trigonometric square identities.

(b)

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\csc A + \csc B}{\cot A + \cot B} \times \frac{\cot A - \cot B}{\cot A - \cot B} &= \frac{(\csc A + \csc B)(\cot A - \cot B)}{\cot^2 A - \cot^2 B} \\ &= \frac{(\csc A + \csc B)(\cot A - \cot B)}{(\csc^2 A - 1) - (\csc^2 B - 1)} \\ &= \frac{(\csc A + \csc B)(\cot A - \cot B)}{\csc^2 A - \csc^2 B} \\ &= \frac{(\csc A + \csc B)(\cot A - \cot B)}{(\csc A + \csc B)(\csc A - \csc B)} \\ &= \frac{\cot A - \cot B}{\csc A - \csc B} \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Method 1: Multiply conjugate

Alternative

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\csc A + \csc B}{\cot A + \cot B} \times \frac{\csc A - \csc B}{\csc A - \csc B} &= \frac{\csc^2 A - \csc^2 B}{(\cot A + \cot B)(\csc A - \csc B)} \\ &= \frac{(1 + \cot^2 A) - (1 + \cot^2 B)}{(\cot A + \cot B)(\csc A - \csc B)} \\ &= \frac{\cot^2 A - \cot^2 B}{(\cot A + \cot B)(\csc A - \csc B)} \\ &= \frac{(\cot A + \cot B)(\cot A - \cot B)}{(\cot A + \cot B)(\csc A - \csc B)} \\ &= \frac{\cot A - \cot B}{\csc A - \csc B} \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Method 2: Multiply opposite denominator

Type 2: Compound Angle Formulae

Example 3

Solve the following equations for \(0 \le x \le \pi\).

(a) \(\tan 6x - \tan 2x - \tan 6x \tan 2x = 1\)

(b) \(\frac{1 + \tan x}{1 - \tan x} = \cot(x + \frac{\pi}{2})\)

(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} \tan 6x - \tan 2x - \tan 6x \tan 2x &= 1 \\ \tan 6x - \tan 2x &= 1 + \tan 6x \tan 2x \\ \frac{\tan 6x - \tan 2x}{1 + \tan 6x \tan 2x} &= 1 \\ \tan(6x - 2x) &= 1 \\ \tan 4x &= 1 \\ 4x &= \frac{\pi}{4} \text{ or } \frac{5\pi}{4} \text{ or } \frac{9\pi}{4} \text{ or } \frac{13\pi}{4} \\ x &= \frac{\pi}{16} \text{ or } \frac{5\pi}{16} \text{ or } \frac{9\pi}{16} \text{ or } \frac{13\pi}{16} \end{aligned} \]

(b)

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{1 + \tan x}{1 - \tan x} &= \cot(x + \frac{\pi}{2}) \\ \frac{\tan \frac{\pi}{4} + \tan x}{1 - \tan \frac{\pi}{4} \tan x} &= \cot(x + \frac{\pi}{2}) \\ \tan(x + \frac{\pi}{4}) &= \tan\left[ \frac{\pi}{2} - (x + \frac{\pi}{2}) \right] \text{ or } \tan(x + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \tan\left[ \frac{3\pi}{2} - (x + \frac{\pi}{2}) \right] \\ x + \frac{\pi}{4} &= x \text{ (rej.) or } x + \frac{\pi}{4} = \pi - x \\ x &= \frac{3\pi}{8} \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Key: \(\cot x = \tan(90^\circ - x)\)

Type 3: Double Angle Formulae

Example 4 [2013-DSE-M2-Q7]

(a) Prove the identity \(\tan x = \frac{\sin 2x}{1 + \cos 2x}\).

(b) Using (a), prove the identity \(\tan y = \frac{\sin 8y \cos 4y \cos 2y}{(1 + \cos 8y)(1 + \cos 4y)(1 + \cos 2y)}\).

(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\sin 2x}{1 + \cos 2x} &= \frac{2\sin x \cos x}{1 + (2\cos^2 x - 1)} \\ &= \frac{2\sin x \cos x}{2\cos^2 x} \\ &= \tan x \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Key: Try to eliminate the 1 when considering which form to use.

(b)

\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{\sin 8y \cos 4y \cos 2y}{(1 + \cos 8y)(1 + \cos 4y)(1 + \cos 2y)} \\ &= \tan 4y \frac{\cos 4y \cos 2y}{(1 + \cos 4y)(1 + \cos 2y)} \\ &= \frac{\sin 4y}{1 + \cos 4y} \times \frac{\cos 2y}{1 + \cos 2y} \\ &= \tan 2y \times \frac{\cos 2y}{1 + \cos 2y} \\ &= \frac{\sin 2y}{1 + \cos 2y} \\ &= \tan y \end{aligned} \]

More Examples

Example 2

It is given that \(\csc A + \cot A = k\).

(a) Express \(\csc A - \cot A\) in terms of \(k\).

(b) If \(\csc A\) and \(\cot A\) are the distinct roots of the quadratic equation \(9x^2 - 27x + p = 0\), where \(p\) is a constant, find the values of \(k\) and \(p\).

(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} \csc^2 A - \cot^2 A &= 1 \\ (\csc A + \cot A)(\csc A - \cot A) &= 1 \\ \csc A - \cot A &= \frac{1}{k} \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Key: Instead of squaring both sides, the conjugates have a difference of reciprocals.

(b)

\[ \begin{aligned} \csc A + \cot A &= k = \frac{27}{9} \\ k &= 3 \\ \begin{cases} \csc A + \cot A = 3 \\ \csc A - \cot A = \frac{1}{3} \end{cases} \\ \text{Solving, } \csc A &= \frac{5}{3}, \cot A = \frac{4}{3} \\ \frac{p}{9} &= \csc A \cot A = \frac{20}{9} \\ p &= 20 \end{aligned} \]

Example 5 [2022-DSE-M2-Q2]

(a) Let \(\frac{\pi}{4} < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}\). Prove the identity \(\frac{\tan \theta}{1 - \cot \theta} + \frac{\cot \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} = 1 + \sec \theta \csc \theta\).

(b) Solve the equation \(\frac{\tan \theta}{1 - \cot \theta} + \frac{\cot \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} = 5\).

(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{\tan \theta}{1 - \cot \theta} + \frac{\cot \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} \\ &= \frac{\tan^2 \theta}{\tan \theta - 1} + \frac{1}{\tan \theta - \tan^2 \theta} \\ &= \frac{\tan^2 \theta}{\tan \theta - 1} - \frac{1}{\tan \theta(\tan \theta - 1)} \\ &= \frac{\tan^3 \theta - 1}{\tan \theta(\tan \theta - 1)} \\ &= \frac{(\tan \theta - 1)(\tan^2 \theta + \tan \theta + 1)}{\tan \theta(\tan \theta - 1)} \\ &= \frac{\sec^2 \theta + \tan \theta}{\tan \theta} \\ &= 1 + \frac{\sec^2 \theta}{\tan \theta} \\ &= 1 + \frac{\sec^2 \theta \cos \theta}{\sin \theta} \\ &= 1 + \sec \theta \csc \theta \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Key: When simplifying reciprocals, we can multiply on both numerator and denominator.

(b)

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\tan \theta}{1 - \cot \theta} + \frac{\cot \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} &= 5 \\ 1 + \sec \theta \csc \theta &= 5 \\ \frac{1}{\cos \theta \sin \theta} &= 4 \\ \frac{2}{2 \cos \theta \sin \theta} &= 4 \\ \frac{2}{\sin 2\theta} &= 4 \\ \sin 2\theta &= \frac{1}{2} \\ 2\theta &= \pi - \frac{\pi}{6} \\ \theta &= \frac{5\pi}{12} \end{aligned} \]
🔑 Key: When there is \(\cos x \sin x\) usually it is double angle formula.

Exercise 1 [Level 1]

It is given that \(\tan \theta + \sec \theta = \frac{2}{5}\). By considering \(\tan \theta - \sec \theta\), find \(\tan \theta\) and \(\csc \theta\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 1 + \tan^2 \theta &= \sec^2 \theta \\ (\sec \theta + \tan \theta)(\sec \theta - \tan \theta) &= 1 \\ \frac{2}{5}(\sec \theta - \tan \theta) &= 1 \\ \sec \theta - \tan \theta &= \frac{5}{2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{cases} \tan \theta + \sec \theta = \frac{2}{5} \\ \tan \theta - \sec \theta = -\frac{5}{2} \end{cases} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Solving, } \tan \theta &= -\frac{21}{20}, \sec \theta = \frac{29}{20} \\ \tan \theta < 0 \text{ and } \sec \theta > 0 &\text{ implies } \csc \theta < 0. \\ \csc \theta &= -\frac{29}{21} \end{aligned} \]
