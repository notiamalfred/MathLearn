Question 2

If f(2x + 1) = 4x² + 2x + 3, then f(2x) =

A. 4x² − 2x − 3
B. 4x² − 2x + 3
C. 4x² + 2x − 3
D. 4x² + 2x + 3

Method 1

Let u = 2x + 1, x = (u − 1)/2 f(u) = 4((u − 1)/2)² + 2((u − 1)/2) + 3 = u² − u + 3 f(2x) = (2x)² − (2x) + 3 = 4x² − 2x + 3

Method 2

Observe that f(2x + 1) = (2x)² + (2x) + 3 and 2x = (2x + 1) − 1.

f(2x) = (2x − 1)² + (2x − 1) + 3 = 4x² − 2x + 3

Method 3

Sub x = 1, f(2x) = f(2), at the same time for f(2x + 1), sub x = 0.5, so f(2 × 0.5 + 1) = 5. Option A: −1, Option B: 5, Option C: 3, Option D: 9.

Application Type 1 — Area

Example — ABCDE figure

In figure 2, ABCDE is formed by a right-angled triangle ABE and a rectangle BCDE, where AB = 3x cm and AE = 4x cm.

(a) Express the length of CD in terms of x.

BE = √((3x)² + (4x)²) = 5x cm (pyth theorem) ∴ CD = BE = 5x cm

(b) If the perimeter of the figure is 96 cm, express the area of rectangle BCDE in terms of x.

BC + ED + 3x + 4x + 5x = 96 2BC = 96 − 12x BC = 48 − 6x Area of BCDE = (48 − 6x)(5x) = (−30x² + 240x) cm²

(c) Find the maximum area of the figure.

Area of the figure = (1/2)(3x)(4x) + (−30x² + 240x) = (−24x² + 240x)

★ Whenever the question word hints "maximum"/"minimum", usually it is related to completing square.

= −24(x² − 10x + 5² − 5²) = −24[(x − 5)² − 25] = [−24(x − 5)² + 600] cm² ∴ The maximum area of the figure is 600 cm²

Application Type 2 — Coordinate Plane

Example — ΔABD area

The graph of y = −x² − 10x − 16 cuts the x-axis at A and B. D is a point on the curve above the x-axis.

(a) Find the coordinates of A and B.

x = −8 or x = −2

The coordinates of A and B are (−8, 0) and (−2, 0) respectively.

(b) Cathy claims that the area of ΔABD does not exceed 27 sq units. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

y = −x² − 10x − 16 = −(x² + 10x + 5² − 5²) − 16 = −(x + 5)² + 9 AB = [−2 − (−8)] = 6 units Maximum area of ΔABD (1/2) × 6 × 9 sq units = 27 sq units

The area of ΔABD does not exceed 27 sq units.
Cathy's claim is correct.
