Glossary of Key Terms

Every definition from the notes, in one place.

Discriminant (Δ)

The expression Δ = b² − 4ac for a quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0.

Completing Square

Rewriting a quadratic expression ax² + bx + c in the form a(x − h)² + k.

Sum of roots

For ax² + bx + c = 0, α + β = −b/a.

Product of roots

For ax² + bx + c = 0, αβ = c/a.

Complex number

A number of the form a + bi, where a and b are real numbers and i = √(−1).

Conjugate

The conjugate of a + bi is a − bi.

General form (quadratic)

y = ax² + bx + c. Shows opening, axis of symmetry and y-intercept.

Vertex form (quadratic)

y = a(x − h)² + k. Shows vertex (h, k) and axis of symmetry x = h.

Intercept form (quadratic)

y = a(x − α)(x − β). Shows x-intercepts α and β.

Axis of symmetry

A vertical line that passes through the vertex of the parabola and lies exactly midway between the two x-intercepts (if they exist). Equation: x = −b/(2a).

Centroid (G)

The point where the three medians of a triangle meet. Always lies inside the triangle. Coordinates: ((x_a + x_b + x_c)/3, (y_a + y_b + y_c)/3). AG : GD = 2 : 1.

Orthocenter (H)

The point where the three altitudes of a triangle meet. Inside for acute, at the right-angle vertex for right-angled, outside for obtuse.

Circumcenter (O)

The center of the circumscribed circle, formed by the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors. OA = OB = OC (radii).

Incenter (I)

The center of the inscribed circle, formed by the intersection of the angle bisectors. DI = EI = FI (radii). pr = 2A. Coordinates: ((ax_a + bx_b + cx_c)/(a + b + c), (ay_a + by_b + cy_c)/(a + b + c)).

Euler's line

For all triangles, the circumcenter O, the centroid V and the orthocenter H lie on the same line.

Perpendicular Bisector Property

If PQ is a perpendicular bisector of BC, then any points on PQ can form isosceles sides with BC (e.g. PB = PC and OB = OC).

Angle Bisector Property

If BI is an angle bisector of ABC with AB ⊥ DI and BC ⊥ IF, then DI = IF.

Angle Bisector Theorem

BM/MC = AB/AC, similarly for other two pairs.

i (imaginary unit)

Definition: i = √(−1). Also i^(4n + r) = i^r for non-negative integers n and r.
