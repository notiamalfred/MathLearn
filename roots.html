Sum and Product of Roots

α + β = −b/a and αβ = c/a.

Basics Type 1 Type 2 Type 3

Basics

Formulas and identities

For a quadratic equation that is in form of ax² + bx + c = 0:

(x − α)(x − β) = 0 x² − (α + β)x + αβ = 0 … (1) ax² + bx + c = 0 x² + (b/a)x + (c/a) = 0 … (2) ∴ α + β = −b/a, αβ = c/a

Three useful identities

1. α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ 2. (α − β)² = (α + β)² − 4αβ 3. α − β = √[(α + β)² − 4αβ]  (for α > β) = −√[(α + β)² − 4αβ]  (for α < β)

Type 1 — Forming Quadratic Equations with Roots

Example 1

If the roots of the equation x² + √3 x − 4 = 0 are α and β, form a quadratic equation in t whose roots are α − β and β − α.

Step 1: write down the sum and product of roots of the original equation.

α + β = −√3 / 1 = −√3 αβ = −4 / 1 = −4

Step 2: find the new sum and product of roots in terms of the original one.

New sum of roots = (α − β) + (β − α) = 0 New product of roots = (α − β)(β − α) = −(α − β)² = −[(α + β)² − 4αβ] = −[(−√3)² − 4(−4)] = −19

Step 3: express the answer in terms of the format t² − (sum of roots)t + (product of roots) = 0.

The required quadratic equation: t² − 19 = 0.

Type 2 — Making Use of the Unknown with Given Roots

Example 2

It is given that α and β are the roots of the quadratic equation x² + mx + n = 0; α + 2 and β + 2 are the roots of the equation x² + nx + m = 0. Find the values of m and n.

★ Step 1: list the original and new sum/product of roots.

α + β = −m, αβ = n ∴ (α + 2) + (β + 2) = −n α + β + 4 = −n −m + 4 = −n m − n = 4 … (1)

★ Step 2: Substitute the values from original sum/product of roots to the new one.

(α + 2)(β + 2) = m αβ + 2(α + β) + 4 = m n + 2(−m) + 4 = m 3m − n = 4 … (2) (2) − (1): 2m = 0 m = 0

By substituting m = 0 into (1), we have:

0 − n = 4 n = −4

Example 3

If α and β are the roots of the quadratic equation x² − 5x + p = 0, then α² + 5β =

A. 10 − p
B. 15 − p
C. 20 − p
D. 25 − p

★ … are the roots →

  1. Sub to the equation
  2. Apply sum/product of roots

For those with different powers, try to reduce them into same powers (1 or 2).

α² − 5α + p = 0 α² = 5α − p α² + 5β = 5α + 5β − p = 5(5) − p = 25 − p

MC: Sub a value to p and then press calculator.

e.g. When p = 4, x² − 5x + 4 = 0 ⇒ x = 1 or x = 4.

α² + 5β = 1² + 5(4) = 21 (D)

Type 3 — Applications of Sum and Product of Roots in Graphs

Example 4

The figure shows the graph of y = −4x² + 12x − (k + 3). It cuts the x-axis at two points A and B, and OA : OB = 1 : 5. Find the value of k.

Set up unknowns to represent the roots.

Let (α, 0) and (β, 0) be the coordinates of A and B respectively.

∵ OA : OB = 1 : 5

Note that OA and OB are distances which are equal to the roots.

∴ α and 5α are roots of −4x² + 12x − (k + 3) = 0.

∴ α + 5α = −12 / −4 and α · 5α = −(k + 3) / −4 6α = 3 and 20α² = k + 3 α = 1/2 and 20(1/2)² = k + 3 k = 2
